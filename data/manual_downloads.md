# Manual downloads (optional, stretch S4)

These brochures and datasheets are linked from FOSS product pages but served from `storage.pardot.com`, whose robots.txt disallows automated access, so they were **not** downloaded automatically. You can open them in a browser and save the ones you want into `data/raw/pdfs/`, then add a row to `data/sources.tsv` (kind `pdfs`, the URL below).

Priority if you only grab a few: the segment brochures and portfolio overviews (Dairy, Grain, Meat, Laboratories, Raw Milk Testing, Wine) and the Best-practice sample preparation white paper.
